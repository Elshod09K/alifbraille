AlifBraille

SXSW EDU Student Impact Challenge 2027 · Elshod Kenjayev, Tashkent, Uzbekistan

A free, open-source web tool that turns Uzbek Latin school texts and exam questions into correct, print-ready Braille in seconds, and reads it aloud cell by cell so sighted teachers who can't read Braille can check every sheet.

  • 393 automated tests passing
  • ~4 ms per 500 characters
  • Runs offline in the browser
  • Marburg Medium, 2.5 mm dot pitch
  • MIT license, $0 for schools
